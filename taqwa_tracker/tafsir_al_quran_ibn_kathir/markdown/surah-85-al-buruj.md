The Interpretation of the Word Burij
Allah swears by the heaven and its Burij. The Burij are the
giant stars, as Allah says,
a IS ek SES CE TE Se Sh HCD
Blessed is He Who has placed in the heaven Buriij, and has
placed therein a great lamp (the sun), and a moon giving
light.> (25:61)
Ibn ‘Abbas, Mujahid, Ad-Dahhak, Al-Hasan, Qatadah and As-
Suddi, all said, “Al-Burij are the stars.”"! Al-Minhal bin ‘Amr
said,

Ibn Jarir chose the view that it means the positions of the sun
and the moon, which are twelve Burij. The sun travels through
each one of these “Burj” (singular of Burd) in one month. The
moon travels through each one of these Burj in two-and-a-third
days, which makes a total of twenty-eight positions, and it is
hidden for two nights.'!
