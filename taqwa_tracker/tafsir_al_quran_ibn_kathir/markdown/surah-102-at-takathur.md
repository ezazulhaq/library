The Result of Loving the World and Heedlessness of the
Hereafter

Allah says that all are preoccupied by love of the world, its
delights and its adornments, and this distracts you from
seeking the Hereafter and desiring it. This delays you until
death comes to you and you visit the graves, thus becoming
its inhabitants.

(Narrations that soften the Heart) from Anas bin Malik, who
