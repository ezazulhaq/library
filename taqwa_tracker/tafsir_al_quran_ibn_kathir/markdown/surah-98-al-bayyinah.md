Mentioning the Worse and Best of Creation and Their
Recompense

Allah informs of what will happen to the wicked disbelievers
among the People of the Scripture and the idolators who
oppose the Allah’s divinely revealed Books and the Prophets
whom He sent. He says that they will be in the fire of Hell on
the Day of Judgement and they will abide therein forever. This
means that they will remain in it and they will have no way
out of it and they will not cease being in it.
