Wicked People

Allah the Exalted ordered investigating the news that sinners
and the wicked bring, to make sure of its authenticity.

decision is based on it, regardless of whether the information
is true or not, the authorities will be taking the lead of the
sinners. Allah the Exalted and Most Honored forbade taking
the path of the corrupted and sinners. This is why groups of
the scholars of Hadith refuse to accept narrations from
narrators whose reliability is unknown, for they might be from
among the wicked people, in reality.
