were revealed about Abu Bakr As-Siddiq. Some of them even
mentioned that there is a consensus among the Qur’anic
commentators concerning this. There is no doubt that he is
included in the meaning of these Aydt, and that he is the
most deserving of the Ummah to be described with these
characteristics in general, for indeed, the wording of these
Ayat is general. As in Allah’s saying,
